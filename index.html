TapFour

You know the rhythm your chords should hit, but you can't play the chords, and dragging blocks around a piano roll kills the vibe. Type your BPM and 4 chords, then tap the rhythm on any one key, Space, or a pad over a real band. Every tap becomes your chord for that bar, voice-led on a real piano. How long you hold sets the length, how hard you hit sets the velocity, and how high you tap sets how high the voicing sits. Every loop pass is a new take. Export a WAV and a chord MIDI file that sit on bar 1. Nothing is uploaded, no AI sounds.

Tap it

One bar of count-in, then tap. Recording loops until you stop it, and every 4-bar pass becomes its own take. Keys: Space or K = mid voicing, J = low, L = high. A MIDI keyboard or pad controller works too: any note is a tap, velocity and the sustain pedal are kept.

Chairs

Play along live

Velocity works from a MIDI controller. The sustain pedal (CC64) holds chords and notes. Notes never start or stop the band. In note modes, A W S E D F T G Y H U J K play C to C and Z / X change octave. ● Record captures Tap mode.
